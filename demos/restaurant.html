Our kitchen

No gas. No shortcuts.

Everything passes over charcoal or it doesn't leave the pass. Bread is baked to order, the yoghurt is hung overnight, and the spice blends are ground in-house every Tuesday.

We buy from four farms and one fisherman. When they have a bad week, the menu changes — which is rather the point.
